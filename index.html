< ABOUT.ME />

I'm a machine learning scientist at Apple, working on the kinds of models that generate, understand, and occasionally surprise you.

I got my Ph.D. in Statistics from Purdue in 2022, where I worked with Professor Michael Zhu on score-based generative models -- basically teaching neural networks to turn one image into another (turns out that's hard). Before that, M.S. from Wisconsin-Madison, B.S. from Nankai University.

These days I'm into diffusion models, LLMs, multi-modal systems, and figuring out how to make all of them behave through alignment and reinforcement learning.
